Card Counter

JUN 2024 · TOOL
OPENCV · PYTESSERACT
VIDEO NOT AVAILABLE :c

NOTES

Built for a professor’s research. Take a picture of a card, OpenCV finds and cleans it up, PyTesseract reads it, and the app tells you how many of that card are left in the deck.

My first real lesson in how much of computer vision is preprocessing rather than the clever bit at the end.
