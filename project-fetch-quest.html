Fetch Quest

FALL 2024 · GAME
GADIG · GODOT · GDSCRIPT
VIDEO NOT AVAILABLE :c

NOTES

A dog sets out to return a stick to its owner, finds a legendary sword along the way, and ends up saving a kingdom from a growing gloom. Puzzles, dungeons, the most grandiose fetch quest of our time.

This was the GADIG game for Fall 2024, and the semester we moved the whole club from Unity over to Godot. I took a more administrative role on this one, which mostly meant making sure eighty people with different schedules shipped the same game.
